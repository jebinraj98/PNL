Bagel daily P&L

Realized profit and loss is worked out from your own buys, sells and settlements (average-cost method) and dated by the day each one happened, so a position bought days ago and cashed out today counts today. Losing positions you never redeemed are counted on the end date of their market. Combos count when you cash them out or they settle, and open ones are not counted. Click any day to see what is inside. Updates every 2 minutes while this page is open.
